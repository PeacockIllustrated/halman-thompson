All metal bath panels from Halman Thompson are made by hand using traditional methods.

They are available in a range of finishes, including:

  • Berkshire finish – A rich and decadent bronzed brass finish that would look good anywhere

  • Hertfordshire finish – Signature aged copper finish with areas of verdigris throughout
  • Northumberland finish – Luxurious swirled aged copper finish with verdigris for colour
  • Natural copper finish – Classic natural copper finish with plenty of warmth

Handmade metal bath panels

Every metal bath panel we make is created by hand, to order. You can specify the exact colour, finish and texture and our artisans will create the panel.

We begin by laser cutting the metal to the exact size you requested. We then prepare the metal for finishing and artificially age it depending on the finish you ordered.

Aged copper and brass finishes use a specialist ageing process we can fully control. As soon as the metal bath panel reaches the desired look, we stop the process.

We can then seal the bath panel in transparent lacquer. This stops the ageing process and preserves the patina of the metal.

Aged metal bath panels will all be sealed in lacquer. Natural copper and brass bath panels can be optionally sealed in lacquer.

Caring for your metal bath panel

One of the many amazing properties of copper and brass is the ease of maintenance.

The lacquer protects the metal from moisture, cleaning products and chemicals.

All you need to do to keep it clean is use warm soapy water and a soft cloth. That’s it!

All metal bath panels from Halman Thompson come with full care instructions so you’ll know exactly what to do.
