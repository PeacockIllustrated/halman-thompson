Hand crafted metal bar tops

We create all metal bar tops by hand, to order. Our team of experienced artisans work the metal in ways only an expert can, artificially ageing where required, laser cutting to shape and covering with a clear coat of lacquer to preserve the finish.

All aged copper and brass bar tops will be sealed in lacquer to stop the ageing process and to protect the look. Natural copper and brass tops can be optionally lacquered or left as bare metal.

Practical metal bar tops

Metal bar tops from Halman Thompson can be prepared ready for you to fit onto a bar or be fitted for you in our workshop. Either way, the bar top will be prepared and ready for use.

Caring for your metal bar top is simple.

Use a soft cloth and warm, soapy water to remove dirt and debris. That’s it!

Avoid using abrasives or chemicals and your bar top will be looking just as good for many years to come!
