Hand crafted metal cooker hoods

Metal cooker hoods are made to measure not just the dimensions of your cooker but also your desired patina and finish.

Metal cooker hood finishes include:

  • Cambridgeshire finish – Natural milled brass finish with a light, friendly feel
  • Somerset finish – Deep, rich and decadent aged brass finish
  • Wiltshire finish – Aged brass finish that is always revealing more
  • Natural brass finish – Natural brass finish that lets its awesome beauty shine through
  • Ayrshire finish – Speckled aged copper finish with plenty of vibrant character
  • Cheshire finish – Subtle aged copper finish with waves or darker tones
  • Hertfordshire finish – Intriguing aged copper finished with distinct areas of verdigris
  • Natural copper finish – All the beauty, character and luxury of natural copper
  • Northumberland finish – Aged copper finish with a unique, complex appeal

Creating your metal cooker hood

All metal cooker hoods are created by hand, to order. Each begins with a brass or copper sheet with a maximum size of 1000mm x 2000mm.

We precision cut the shape of your hood using a laser and your exact dimensions. We then shape the metal into shape and finish to an exceptional standard. If you order an aged metal hood, we will use our specialist ageing processes to deliver the desired look and then stop ageing. Finally, we can then add a tough transparent lacquer to prevent further ageing and protect the metal.

All aged metal cooker hoods will be lacquered. Natural brass or copper can be optionally lacquered.

As everything is made by hand, every piece will be completely unique. It will also fit perfectly, we guarantee it!

Caring for your metal cooker hood

Caring for your metal cooker hood is very straightforward. Simple wipe down with a soft cloth and warm soapy water.

No specialist cleaning products and no complicated processes here. Just good old fashioned soap and water!

Full care instructions will be included with every order.
