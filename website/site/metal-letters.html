Metal Letters

Handcrafted metal letters

Copper metal letters

Copper metal letters offer an attractive feel to any room. Whether for the home or a commercial space, copper adds a unique character that makes everyone feel welcome.

Copper metal letters are available as natural copper or aged copper . Aged copper is artificially aged and then sealed in lacquer to protect it and stop the ageing process.

Natural copper can be protected by lacquer too if required.

Brass metal letters

Brass metal letters provide a chic feel that is hard to compete with. Few other metals or materials offer the same depth and timeless appeal of brass, which is why it’s still popular today.

Brass metal letters can be provided as natural brass or aged brass . Aged brass is artificially aged in the same way as copper and then sealed in lacquer to stop ageing and to protect the finish.

Natural brass letters can also be optionally finished in lacquer.

All metal letters can be laser cut to size and prepared ready for installation.

Bespoke metal letters

Choose your own font, choose your own size (max 1000mm X 2000mm) and choose your own finish.

You can spell out your name, your brand name, your address or just the number of your home. Letters and numbers are uniquely flexible, so can be used in any way you choose.

We regularly provide metal letters to businesses, bars, cafes, hair salons, garden centres, shops and other premises as well as the home.

You can even install your metal letters outdoors if you like. Just tell us when you’re ordering so we can prepare them for life in the outside world.

Metal has always been popular in interior design and work just as well outdoors. We use brass and copper because they exhibit amazing character, a depth of colour and unique patina whether you buy aged or natural.

Either way, with metal letters from Halman Thompson will deliver the look you want with the ageless appeal of copper or brass.
