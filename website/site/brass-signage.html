Brass Signage

Brass signage is a stylish and durable choice for businesses looking to make a lasting impression. Whether for a corporate office, retail shop, hotel, or restaurant, bespoke brass signs offer an elegant yet professional aesthetic. From brass nameplates for office doors to brass plaques for commemorative displays, this versatile material is perfect for a range of business applications.

Brass signage is also ideal for directional and informational signs, regulatory notices, real estate signage, and branded business plaques. At Halman Thompson, we specialise in crafting bespoke brass signs tailored to your exact requirements.

Using high-quality brass sheets, we offer a range of options, from natural brass to aged brass styles, allowing you to achieve the perfect look. For added durability, our brass signs can also be coated with a clear lacquer to preserve their appearance, ensuring minimal maintenance. Every sign is made to order, offering a truly personalised solution for your business.

Brass Signage

Timeless Durability

Brass signage is not just about aesthetics—it is also designed to last. Our high-quality brass sheets are strong, durable, and weather-resistant, making them ideal for both indoor and outdoor use. Whether mounted on an office wall, displayed on a shopfront, or used for directional signage in a hotel, brass signs offer a long-lasting solution.

To maintain their pristine look, you can opt for a transparent lacquer finish that protects against natural tarnishing, ensuring your brass signage retains its appeal with minimal maintenance. With this protective coating, there is no need for regular polishing, allowing you to focus on running your business without worrying about the upkeep and maintenance of your signage.
