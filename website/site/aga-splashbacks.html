AGA Splashbacks

Splashbacks provide the ideal opportunity to express your creativity while balancing practicality. Copper and brass are hard wearing, ideal for kitchens and can be made to look amazing. What more could you ask?

AGA splashbacks from Halman Thompson takes those features and turns them up a notch to complement the beauty of the AGA stove.

The AGA has been around for over a century and brass and copper have been around for much longer.

The combination brings timeless appeal with a touch of class, ideal for any kitchen design!

All AGA splashbacks from Halman Thompson are created by hand, to order and are available in any shape or size you require.

Choose your finish, place your order and we’ll take care of the rest!

Please note: our pieces are suitable for use behind non-steam cookers only. It is important that the material is not exposed to direct contact with steam or excessive heat, as this will damage the surface finish.

CUSTOM AGA SPLASHBACKS

The AGA cooker is an amazing piece of kitchen hardware, and only something of the quality and character of brass or copper can do it justice.

Forget tiles or paint, only something with the same quality finish and ageless appeal will do!

All AGA splashbacks from Halman Thompson are created by hand. You choose the colour, depth and patina of the piece and we’ll do the rest.

Each AGA splashback begins as a sheet of copper or brass. It is then precision cut with a laser to the exact dimensions and prepared for finishing.

Aged AGA splashbacks are then aged using a specialist process until it matches your requirements. Ageing is then stopped and the splashback is coated with a special lacquer.

Natural brass and copper splashbacks and be left as bare metal or protected by the same lacquer.

LOOKING AFTER YOUR AGA SPLASHBACK

One of the many benefits of copper and brass AGA splashbacks is its ease of use.

Once installed, caring for your splashback is simple. Just wipe down with a clean cloth and warm soapy water and your piece will look as good as new!

Full care instructions are included with every order.
