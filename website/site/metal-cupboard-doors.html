Hand crafted metal cupboard doors

There’s nothing quite like brass or copper to transform a kitchen. Whether as a practical upgrade or complete redesign, metal cupboard doors make a statement while delivering practicality.

All metal cupboard doors begin as sheets of copper or brass. Our expert artisans laser cut each sheet to exact dimensions and prepare them for finishing.

Aged copper or brass cupboard doors are then artificially aged using a specialist process. As we perform this by hand, we can deliver the exact colour, depth and patina you’re looking for.

Once complete, we stop the ageing process and finish the door with a tough, transparent lacquer.

Natural brass or copper cupboard doors can be left as bare metal or protected by the same lacquer.

Once finished, we can provide the doors as is or fit to wood ready for installation. It’s entirely up to you!

Caring for your metal cupboard doors

If you need another reason to consider metal cupboard doors for your kitchen, just consider how easy they are to look after.

Wash down regularly with a soft cloth and warm soapy water to keep them looking as good as new.

No specialist processes or solutions. No special cleaners. Just soapy water and a minute of your time.

Full care instructions will be included with every order.
