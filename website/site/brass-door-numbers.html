Bespoke Brass Door Numbers

There is something about brass that is pure class. The colour, texture or ageless appearance. Whatever it is, brass is an incredibly versatile metal used in all manner of ways, including creating attractive bespoke brass door numbers.

Bespoke brass door numbers enable you to number your house, your building, your office, your restaurant or whatever you like. Install them outside, or inside, large or small. It’s an exceptionally flexible way of decorating a property.

You’ll often see brass door numbers in hotels, on the outside of office buildings or upmarket shops or restaurants. You can see them inside buildings as floor numbers or just as a decorative statement.

Bespoke brass door numbers are available in natural or aged brass and are laser cut to the exact shape and size you need.

HANDCRAFTED BRASS DOOR NUMBERS

All brass door numbers from Halman Thompson are handcrafted to order.

We laser cut your door number from a sheet of brass using the exact size, shape and font you require.

Our skilled team will then artificially age the brass if you’re going for the aged look, until it’s perfect. We’ll then seal it in tough, transparent lacquer to protect it from the elements.

Natural brass finishes can be left as is, or protected by the same lacquer for longevity.

Just let us know when you contact us whether it will be used indoors or outdoors and we can advise on the best option.

SIZING YOUR DOOR NUMBERS

You can have your door numbers in any size up to a maximum of 1000mm X 2000mm per number.

We laser cut the sheets into the size, shape and font you order and finish them to an exceptional standard so you can be proud to display your number.

INSTALLATION AND CARE OF YOUR BRASS DOOR NUMBERS

Installing brass door numbers is very straightforward. You can use epoxy, adhesive or fixings if you want your numbers to stand out from the surface.

Once installed, caring for the door numbers is easy. Simply wash with warm soapy water and soft cloth. The lacquer we use is designed to protect your numbers as much as possible and requires very little maintenance.

Full installation and care instructions are provided with every order.
