Handcrafted Metal Coffee Tables

Our metal coffee tables are handmade with our natural copper or brass sheets, aged and finished by our artisan craftsmen to guarantee quality every time. They are a stylish and modern way to add a bit of flair to your living space. Whether you’re looking for a simple accent piece or a more bold and eye-catching piece of furniture, metal coffee tables are a great choice. These aged copper and brass coffee tables come in a selection of finishes, so you’re sure to find one that will fit your décor and taste. Metal coffee tables offer a great deal of versatility since they can be used in any room in the house. In the living room, they can serve as a modern centrepiece, or as a side table for drinks or other items. In the bedroom, they can be used to hold a lamp or books. Even in commercial spaces such as cafés, offices and waiting rooms, they can provide a stylish and modern alternative to the traditional wooden coffee table. Available in small, medium, and large sizes, these coffee tables can be tailored to suit individual preferences and spatial requirements. The use of copper or brass materials adds an undeniable touch of luxury and warmth to the overall aesthetic, while also infusing durability that ensures longevity. Every aspect is carefully considered during the production process, from the flawless construction of the sturdy frame to the seamless addition of intricate patterns, texture and verdigris on the table surface. Whether placed in a contemporary living room or complementing an eclectic interior design scheme, these bespoke metal coffee tables promise both functionality and timeless beauty in any setting. Caring For Your Metal Coffee Table Each of our aged brass and copper metal coffee tables is sealed and protected with a strong matte or gloss lacquer of your choosing. They are designed to last for years and maintenance is very simple. Just use warm soapy water and a soft cloth to remove dirt and debris from the metal table top. But don’t worry, all our metal coffee tables come with full care instructions to make the most of your bespoke product. All the metal wall art we sell is protected by a strong lacquer but they can benefit from soft cleaning. Use soapy water and a soft cloth to remove dirt or debris from the metal, and don’t use abrasives or chemical cleaners as they can break down the protective lacquer. We provide care instructions with every piece of metal wall art we sell so you’ll know exactly what to do. Contact the Halman Thompson team for a free, personalised quotation!

Metal Coffee Table Finishes

Our metal coffee tables come in brass and copper styles, with a range of finishes available.

Brass coffee tables are available in the following finishes…

  • Wiltshire : Our original aged brass look, featuring mottled golden tones of shining and muted metal.
  • Cambridgeshire : A design classic: milled natural brass with a smooth, matte finish.
  • Natural Brass : Gleaming, glamorous brass. Golden metal at its very best.
  • Somerset : AKA our ‘Stained Glass Aged Brass’, this show-stopping, crackle-effect brass features shards of golden, imbued with subtle hints of pink, blue and green.
  • Hampshire : A beautifully burnished, antique-style, rustic brass with a matte finish that accentuates deeper tones.
  • Berkshire : A delicately burnished, antique-effect brass with soft, bronzed tones.
  • Brushed Brass : Lightly burnished for a subtle weathered effect, with a timeless ‘brushed’ finish.
  • Flintshire : Heavily speckled, with patterns of darker aged brass over a brighter, natural brass background.

Copper coffee tables are available in these finishes…

  • Cheshire : Aged copper with a rich array of autumnal tones from a palette of russet, cinnamon and rose.
  • Northumberland : Maximum impact patinated copper with dramatic whorls of verdigris in shades of turquoise and jade.
  • Natural Copper : Original, pinkish copper, ready to gleam and glow in your space.
  • Hertfordshire : Known in-house as ‘Stained Glass Copper’, this impactful finish mixes natural copper tones with crackles and splashes of vibrant verdigris.
  • Ayrshire : A delicately-aged effect, Ayrshire combines the rustic tones of natural copper with muted speckles of blue-green verdigris.
  • B rushed Copper : Bright, shiny natural copper with a stylish ‘brushed’ finish.
  • Pembrokeshire : Gleaming aged copper with deep earthy tones like umber, burnt sienna and salmon pink. All the added interest of aged metal with the glossy finish of natural copper.
