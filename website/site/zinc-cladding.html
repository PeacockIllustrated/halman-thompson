Handcrafted Zinc Cladding Panels

Zinc cladding is an incredibly versatile cladding material ideal for both building and decorating projects. It can be cut to any shape or size and prepared to your exact specifications.

Every zinc cladding panel from Halman Thompson is handcrafted by our experienced artisans and can be cut to any shape or size, prepared to your exact specifications. We use only the highest-quality zinc sheets, applying finishes and detailing by hand to create unique results.

Available in natural or aged zinc finishes, each panel can be customised to suit your specific project. Every piece is finished with a protective coating with either a gloss or matte lacquer as standard, though you can also opt for an unlacquered finish to allow the zinc to develop its own grey patina over time. This means your zinc cladding will be more than just a functional wall cladding – it will be a bespoke feature, designed exclusively for your space.

Whether you’re creating a striking new façade, updating an interior wall, or working on a bespoke commercial project, Halman Thompson’s custom zinc cladding systems offers a premium, handcrafted solution tailored to your needs!

Aged Zinc Cladding

Aged zinc cladding is a particularly striking choice for both contemporary and traditional designs. Our hand-aged finishes range from lightly weathered silver-grey tones to darker, richer antique styles, giving your building or interior feature added depth and character. Popular choices include our lightly aged zinc and antique zinc finishes.

Unlike painted or synthetic materials, aged zinc develops naturally over time. Choosing an unlacquered surface allows the cladding to continue evolving, creating a distinctive patina unique to your project. This makes aged zinc cladding an ideal choice for those who want a finish that tells its own story and enhances with age. Options such as pre-weathered zinc can also be provided for projects where a consistent tone is desired from day one.

Why Choose Zinc Cladding?

Zinc cladding is a strong, durable, and sustainable choice for modern building and design projects. Naturally resistant to corrosion, fire-safe, and requiring very little maintenance, zinc has an impressive lifespan that can extend to around 100 years, making it a cost-effective long-term investment.

Zinc requires relatively little energy to produce and is fully recyclable at the end of its life cycle, making it one of the most eco-friendly materials.

Its lightweight nature makes it quick and easy to install, reducing labour time on site, while its flexibility means it can be used for a wide variety of designs, from sleek zinc façades to intricate detailing. It’s clear to understand the popularity of zinc in construction applications!

For property owners, it offers an affordable, low-maintenance option with lasting performance. For businesses and commercial projects, such as office buildings, retail units, or hospitality venues, zinc cladding provides a durable, visually striking solution that enhances the building’s appearance while keeping long-term costs and upkeep to a minimum.

Compared to alternatives such as aluminium, steel, or stainless steel, zinc offers a softer, more natural grey appearance that blends seamlessly into both rural and urban landscapes. For interiors, it provides a contemporary metallic aesthetic that complements a wide range of design styles.

With its natural beauty, versatility, sustainability, and the availability of different zinc finishes, zinc is an excellent material for achieving a bespoke, practical solution for any cladding project.

Looking After Your Zinc Cladding

Caring for your zinc cladding is simple. Both lacquered and unlacquered finishes only require occasional cleaning with a soft cloth and mild soapy water. Unlacquered zinc will continue to age naturally over time, developing a characterful patina that offers additional protection against the elements.

Avoid using harsh cleaners or abrasive pads, and your cladding will maintain its distinctive appearance for many years. With minimal maintenance, your zinc cladding will remain a standout feature, both indoors and out. Full care instructions are included with every order so you’ll know exactly what to do.
