Bespoke Zinc Tiles for the Home

Every zinc tile we produce is handmade here in the UK using premium quality zinc sheets . Our team applies each finish by hand, whether you choose our lightly aged zinc for a subtle silver patina or the deeper, darker tones of our antique zinc . You can also opt for a lacquered or unlacquered surface.

Our interior zinc tiles are coated with a transparent lacquer as standard to protect the metal from further ageing and maintain a consistent appearance over time. Alternatively, you can opt for an unlacquered tile so the surface will naturally develop and age, adding unique character to your space as it ages.

Zinc tiles for the home are ideal for kitchens, bathrooms, living areas and more. They can be installed vertically or horizontally, used as full wall coverings, borders, splashbacks or eye-catching accent panels. However you decide to use them, they bring a refined, handmade touch to your home.

02

Why Choose Interior Zinc Tiles?

Interior zinc tiles are a fantastic option for homeowners and designers seeking something out of the ordinary. Unlike ceramic or porcelain, zinc offers a cool-toned metallic silver-like finish that catches the light beautifully and adds understated sophistication to a space.

They’re hardwearing, low maintenance, and naturally resist moisture and corrosion, making them ideal for busy kitchens, steamy bathrooms, or in stylish lounges. Zinc is also antibacterial, which makes it a practical and hygienic choice for areas where cleanliness is important.

Our zinc tiles for the home are made to order, ensuring you get a tailored, high-quality finish that will stand the test of time. Zinc is also an environmentally friendly material, thanks to its natural abundance, long lifespan, and low-impact production process.

03

Looking After Your Zinc Tiles

Your interior zinc tiles are easy to look after. For lacquered tiles, just wipe them gently with a soft cloth and mild soapy water. Avoid abrasive sponges and harsh cleaners.

Unlacquered zinc tiles will naturally develop a patina as they age, which is part of their charm. Be sure to wipe spills promptly to avoid unwanted marks, and dust or clean when required with a soft microfibre cloth. With minimal upkeep, your tiles will remain a stunning feature for years to come!
