Size & finish guide

Measuring up

Measure the area in millimetres and enter the exact size — we cut to the millimetre. Measure twice, especially around worktops and sockets.

Need cut-outs for sockets or pipes, or a shape other than a rectangle? Get a bespoke quote .

Which thickness?

0.9mm is our recommended thickness for splashbacks and wall panels.

Thicker sheets are more rigid. If you're unsure which suits your project, ask our team .

Fitting

Pieces arrive ready to fit and can be fixed to most flat surfaces with epoxy resin or liquid nail.

Gloss or matte?

Every piece is sealed in a protective lacquer. Gloss gives a reflective, lively surface; Matte a soft, low-sheen look.

Industrial sophistication and timeless elegance.

Blackened Corten is a bold and contemporary finish that transforms the natural character of steel into a deep charcoal-black surface with subtle tonal variation. Rich in texture and depth, it preserves the organic patina while offering a more refined, architectural aesthetic. Ideal for modern interiors and statement surfaces, this finish brings warmth, durability, and understated sophistication to kitchens, furniture, wall cladding and bespoke joinery

Every piece is sealed with a protective lacquer or crystalline wax to keep the finish as it is. If that layer is worn away by harsh cleaning or heat, the metal will carry on ageing naturally.

  • Clean gently with warm, soapy water
  • Wipe up spills straight awayacidic liquids like vinegar or fruit juice will mark the finish
  • Use heat mats and avoid scratching
  • Re-lacquer if needed
