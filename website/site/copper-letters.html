Copper Letters

Copper letters are designed to make a statement. Whether you’re stating your company name, house or building name, or something else, do it with copper.

Copper letters provide a warm welcome to visitors as well as a design statement. They are available as natural copper or aged copper finishes . All letters are made by hand to order, so you can have whatever you want, in whatever size you want.

Your copper letters will be laser cut from a copper sheet to the exact size, shape and typeface you require. Each letter will then be artificially aged to your desired finish and protected by a tough weatherproof lacquer.

Copper letters can be used indoors or outdoors. Just let us know the intended use when ordering so we can prepare the finish accordingly.

Bespoke copper letters

Copper letters are available in a range of aged copper or natural finishes. Each is applied by hand during manufacture so you can choose whatever colour, texture or patina you like from our range.

Natural copper can be left unlacquered for a simple, luxurious look or protected by a clear lacquer. Aged copper will all be protected by that tough lacquer.

Letters can be any size up to 1000mm x 2000mm as that’s the maximum size sheet we can work with.

Select the letters, typeface, size, colour, patina and we’ll do the rest!

Creating your handcrafted copper letters

The creation process is both simple and complex. All copper letters begin as sheets and will be laser cut to your exact specifications. Once cut and finished, the letters will either be artificially aged or be finished further if natural copper.

Our team of artisans can artificially age your copper letters in one of our range of exquisite finishes. Once aged to your desired look, ageing is stopped and sealed inside a tough, transparent lacquer.

That lacquer can protect the copper from daily use or the weather, depending on where you’re planning to use them.

Caring for your copper letters

Another benefit of using copper is that it’s incredibly low maintenance. Simply wash down with a soft cloth and warm soapy water to keep clean.

The lacquer protects the metal from the environment, so a little care every now and then will be all they need.

Full care instructions will be included with every order.
