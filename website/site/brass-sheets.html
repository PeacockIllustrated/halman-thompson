Brass Sheets

Brass is ageless. Rich, luxurious and one of those metals that surpasses trends and the passage of time. Brass sheet products from Halman Thompson make the most of those features in sheet form or as bespoke products.

We provide brass sheet products designed to your specific requirements. They can be supplied as sheets of almost any size or be turned into bespoke products by our in-house team of artisans.

We can create bar tops , worktops , table tops , backsplashes , wall panels , wall art and anything you can think of. We can supply the brass as sheets or build the entire piece for you. It’s all part of the service!

Brass Sheets

Brass sheet finishes

Our brass sheet products typically come in two distinct finishes. Natural brass that takes everything that’s good about this metal and makes it ready to use. Aged brass that adds some amazing patina and variation to an already lovely metal.

Both brass sheet finishes can be protected by a tough, transparent lacquer. On aged brass this stops the ageing process and can be applied to natural brass too.

Browse finishes on our shop page or choose a finish above to order online.

BESPOKE BRASS SHEET PRODUCTS

Halman Thompson can supply brass as sheets cut to any size you require, in any finish you want.

We can also create your worktop, table top, bar top or numerous other bespoke items. We can cut, mould and fit the brass sheet to wood cut to size and deliver a complete product ready for installation.

We can also create any design in brass. Simply provide an illustration and dimensions and we’ll take care of the rest!

Brass sheet products take the flexibility and ageless quality of this metal and make it fit for modern day. It’s an amazing material to work with and to look at, which is why it has remained so popular.

Contact our sales team or complete the bespoke quote form for a fast, free quotation or to discuss your needs!
