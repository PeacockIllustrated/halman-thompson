Description

Make a bold statement in your living space with a beautiful brass coffee table.

These stunning centrepieces are made to order in any of our brass finishes, both natural and hand-aged. Whether your decor style is vintage, bohemian, maximalist or minimalist, there’s a finish to suit.

As with all our aged metal creations, we will share our progress with you. Only when you are happy with the level of aging achieved will we seal your bespoke piece, using your choice of matte or gloss lacquer.

Every table is hand-made in our studio from brass on MDF, with powder-coated steel legs in black.

Please allow 8-10 weeks for us to make your table.
