8 Navy and Copper Kitchen Ideas

14 Feb 2025 · 4 min read

A navy and copper kitchen is a timeless combination that blends deep, rich tones with warm metallic accents. Whether you’re planning a full kitchen renovation or looking for small upgrades, this colour scheme is a stylish choice that exudes sophistication.

Why Choose a Navy and Copper Kitchen?

Navy blue is a versatile and elegant colour that creates depth and drama in any kitchen. When paired with copper, the contrast is striking yet harmonious. The rich navy tones provide a bold backdrop, while copper adds warmth and a touch of luxury.

Copper isn’t just visually stunning—it’s also highly durable and has natural antimicrobial properties, making it an excellent material for kitchen elements such as sinks, handles, and splashbacks. Whether you prefer a sleek modern kitchen or a more rustic, traditional look, navy and copper kitchens can be tailored to suit your style.

Navy and Copper Kitchen Inspiration

Here are some of our favourite navy and copper kitchen designs to inspire your next project.

1. Bright White and Navy Kitchen with Copper Sink and Handles

A navy and copper kitchen doesn’t have to feel dark. This design keeps the space bright and airy with white walls and worktops, while navy cabinets add depth. The copper sink creates a stunning focal point, complemented by copper handles and pendant lights that tie the look together beautifully.

2. Sleek All-Navy Kitchen with Copper Undermount Sink and Faucet

For a modern and minimalist approach, this kitchen features handleless navy gloss cabinets, putting all the emphasis on the copper accents. The undermount copper sink and matching faucet stand out against the dark cabinetry, giving the space a seamless and high-end feel.

3. Navy Kitchen Cabinets with Aged Copper Splashback

Aged copper splashbacks bring a rustic charm to a navy kitchen. This design contrasts deep blue cabinetry with an oxidised copper splashback, adding texture and warmth. The open white shelving and light wood flooring prevent the space from feeling too dark, while carefully selected crockery enhances the overall aesthetic.

4. Traditional Navy Kitchen with Wooden Worktops

This classic navy kitchen combines wooden worktops with painted navy cupboards and drawers, creating a soft contrast. The natural wood tones mimic the warm hues of copper, making them a perfect match. For an extra touch of elegance, adding copper handles or a copper sink would further enhance the look.

5. Navy Kitchen Chef Block with Copper Handles

A kitchen island or chef block in navy blue can become a statement piece, especially when paired with a natural wooden top. The copper handles elevate the design, while a white cooker and tabletops brighten the space, ensuring it doesn’t feel too heavy.

6. Navy and Copper Kitchen with Moroccan Tiles

If you’re looking for something a little different, this design incorporates Moroccan tiles for a unique, eye-catching feature. The navy cupboards and drawers are complemented by copper handles, a light wood worktop, and matching copper plug sockets for a cohesive and stylish look.

7. Dark Navy Kitchen with Subtle Copper Accents

A dark navy kitchen can feel grand and regal when designed correctly. This sophisticated space features all-navy cabinetry, a marble black splashback, and dark wood floors. The brushed copper handles and pendant lighting add a touch of warmth and elegance, while high ceilings and natural light ensure the space remains open and inviting.

8. Navy Kitchen Island with Copper Worktop

For a striking centrepiece, this design incorporates a navy kitchen island with a bespoke copper worktop. The copper surface reflects light beautifully, making the space feel brighter. Paired with navy cabinets and white walls, it creates a perfect balance between industrial and homely aesthetics.

Navy and Copper Kitchen FAQs

Why choose a navy and copper kitchen?

Navy and copper kitchens are stylish, timeless, and versatile. The deep blue tones provide a dramatic yet calming atmosphere, while copper accents add warmth and sophistication. Plus, copper is naturally antimicrobial and highly durable, making it a practical choice for kitchen elements.

What colours go well in a navy kitchen?

Navy pairs beautifully with a range of colours. White and grey create a crisp contrast, while wood tones add warmth. Copper and brass accents enhance the luxurious feel, while soft pastels or deep jewel tones can introduce a more personalised touch.

Do navy and copper go together?

Absolutely! The cool, dark tones of navy contrast perfectly with the warm glow of copper, creating a balanced and visually striking combination. Whether you go for subtle copper accents or bold copper features, this pairing adds depth and elegance to any kitchen.

What accessories suit a navy kitchen?

Copper accessories are the perfect complement to a navy kitchen. Incorporating copper kitchen taps and handles creates a cohesive look, while copper cookware and utensils add functional decor. Copper light fixtures enhance warmth, and navy and copper small appliances, such as kettles and toasters, tie the theme together seamlessly.
