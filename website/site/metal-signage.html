Bespoke metal signage

Custom Metal Signs

When it comes to custom metal signage, Halman Thompson offers unparalleled versatility and quality. Whether you’re looking for a sign to attract customers, display essential information, or enhance your branding, we create signs that catch attention and stand the test of time.

Our copper and brass signs come in a variety of sizes and finishes, allowing you to personalise every detail of your sign. Choose your preferred font, size, metal, and finish to ensure your sign matches your business’ aesthetic. Add logos, text, borders, and lines for a completely unique design. Whether you prefer to glue mount your sign to a wall, frame it on a free-standing stake, or use pre-drilled holes for screw or wire mounting, our signage is prepared and ready for seamless installation.

Metal signage can be engraved for a sleek look, laser cut for precision text, or crafted with a 3D effect to add depth and texture. Whatever your requirements, Halman Thompson brings your vision to life with bespoke metal signs that combine form and function.

Copper signs

Copper signs are a stunning way to add warmth and sophistication to your business, venue, or space. Available in a range of different styles and finishes, our copper signage can also be aged to create a striking patina for added character. These aged copper signs are then sealed to preserve the finish you choose, ensuring they maintain their exceptional appearance for years to come. Perfect for shopfronts, cafes, and boutique hotels, copper signs offer a natural, classic aesthetic.

Brass signs

Brass signage brings a classic, refined look to any setting. Known for their durability and timeless appeal, brass signs are ideal for various applications. Whether you prefer the gleam of polished brass or the vintage charm of an aged finish, our handcrafted brass signs are designed to impress. They are ideal for shopfronts, heritage buildings, and high-end interiors where sophistication is key.

Personalised metal signs

Halman Thompson offers fully personalised metal signage to suit the unique needs of your business. Customise your sign with your choice of font, size, metal, and finish. Whether you opt for natural or aged copper and brass, our skilled artisans will craft a sign that aligns perfectly with your vision.

If you have something else in mind, why not discuss your specific requirements with our team to explore other design and material options, including corten steel signs, aluminium signs, or stainless steel signs.

Personalised metal signs are ideal for shops, restaurants, hotels, offices, and a wide range of other businesses. They can be used for branding, wayfinding, or adding a touch of sophistication to your premises. Whether you need a sleek, modern look or a rustic, aged aesthetic, we have the expertise to create custom signage that meets your expectations. Let Halman Thompson help you create bespoke business signage that makes an unforgettable impression.

Contact our sales team to discuss your needs or complete our bespoke quote form for a fast, free metal signage quotation.
