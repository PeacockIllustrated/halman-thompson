Brass Stair Risers

Handmade Brass Stair Risers

Halman Thompson creates everything by hand, to order. This gives you full control over the colour, patina and finish of your brass stair risers.

You can select brass stair risers in a range of finishes, including:

  • Natural brass finish – Natural brass with all the beauty and interest you could need
  • Cambridgeshire finish – Impactful milled brass finish with a light colour
  • Somerset finish – Intense aged brass patina with layered colours and textures
  • Wiltshire finish – Light aged brass finish with a lovely speckled effect

CUSTOM BRASS STAIR RISERS

All brass stair risers will be laser cut to size, aged and prepared by hand, and will fit perfectly!

Everything we do here at Halman Thompson is bespoke. We cut every riser by laser to the exact dimensions you need. We’ll then artificially age them and prepare them for use.

We can also finish them using tough, transparent lacquer to help keep them in amazing condition. Lacquer is optional on natural brass stair risers but required on aged brass.

Your brass stair risers can then be fitted using adhesive, epoxy or screwed into place depending on your needs.

CARING FOR YOUR BRASS STAIR RISERS

One of the benefits of brass is that it’s low maintenance, hard wearing and easy to look after.

All you need to do is wash down regularly with warm soapy water to remove scuffs and your riser will look as good as new.

No need for special treatments or chemical cleaners. Simply wash down, wipe off and you’re good to go!

Full care instructions will be included with every order.
