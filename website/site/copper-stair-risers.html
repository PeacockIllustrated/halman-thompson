HANDCRAFTED COPPER STAIR RISERS

Every copper stair riser we create is made by hand to your exact specifications. Our expert team of metalworkers will take your design and turn it into reality.

All copper stair risers begin as a sheet of copper and are laser cut to size. We then age the copper if required or prepare it for use.

Copper ageing is a delicate process but our team has many years of experience with it. We’ll deliver your risers to the exact look and finish you require and ensure all risers have the same depth of character while remaining unique.

We will then protect the riser with a tough, transparent lacquer so it retains those lovely looks before sending them via priority courier.

Installation is simple. You can use adhesive or epoxy or use fixings to secure them in place.

CARING FOR YOUR COPPER STAIR RISERS

Aside from looking amazing, copper stair risers are also easy to maintain. Once securely in place, all they need is a wipe down every now and then with a soft cloth and warm soapy water.

No need for specialist cleaners or harsh abrasives. The lacquer protects the metal and ensures risers are easy to clean.

Full care instructions are included with every order.
