Our selection of brass kitchen products includes:

Brass splashbacks can create genuine impact while providing a practical use in the kitchen. Designed to be beautiful while also protecting walls and providing a tough, stain resistant surface, brass splashbacks are some of our best sellers.

Brass worktops are exceptionally popular within interior design. Whether in the home, a salon, barber shop, bar, bistro or restaurant, nothing makes the same impact as a bespoke brass worktop.

Brass table tops provide the same features and benefits as brass worktops, but in a more customizable form. Any shape, any size, any patina. Anything is possible when you work with Halman Thompson!

Brass bar tops are striking, hard wearing, stain resistant and lovely to look at. What more could a bar need? Whether it’s a bar, restaurant, bistro or somewhere else, natural brass or aged brass bar tops deliver.

Want something other than anonymous pictures in your hallway or entrance? Want to display your house or building number or business name in a stylish but impactful way? Brass wall art is how you do it.

Brass cooker hoods work on their own or can complement a brass splashback or worktop within a kitchen or open space. They are colourful, practical, full of texture and a simple but very effective way to enhance a space.

Brass plinths or kickboards are a subtle, yet very effective way of finishing a kitchen or levelling up an interior. Designed to match your exact specifications, they can be used at home, at work or anywhere you like!

Have an idea you want to turn into brass? Have something specific in mind you would like to create? Halman Thompson can help!

We can take your idea and turn it into reality in brass. Simply contact one of our team to discuss your vision and we’ll do the rest.

Whether it’s a custom brass cupboard door, decorative wall edging, trim for shelf boards or something else, whatever your idea, whatever shape or size, we can help!
