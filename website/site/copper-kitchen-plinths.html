Copper Kitchen Plinths

Copper kitchen plinths, also known as copper kickboards, are the thin strip underneath kitchen cupboards. Usually beneath our notice, this is your chance to elevate them!

Copper kitchen plinths take a usually invisible area of a kitchen and level them up in a big way. Copper is a colourful, characterful and versatile metal, perfect for use as a kickboard.

If you’re looking to make a design statement or to add something a little different to your kitchen, a copper kitchen kickboard could be it!

Everything we do is done by hand and finished to an exceptional standard.

Copper Kitchen Plinths

Bespoke copper kitchen kickboards

As we make every copper kitchen kickboard to order, you can specify the exact look, feel and dimensions. We’ll do the rest.

Copper kitchen plinths are available in the following finishes:

  • Ayrshire finish – Heritage aged copper finish with a comforting depth
  • Cheshire finish – Undulating aged copper finish with a mix of light and dark tones
  • Hertfordshire finish – Amazing aged copper finish with vibrant exposed verdigris
  • Natural copper finish – All the beauty, timeless appeal and warmth of natural copper
  • Northumberland finish – Spectacular aged copper finish with swirls of colour throughout

Creating your bespoke copper kitchen plinth

All copper kitchen plinths are created by hand by experienced artisans who are able to bring your designs to life!

All copper kitchen kickboards begin as copper sheets measuring 1000mm x 2000mm.

We precision cut using a laser to your exact dimensions and finish to a very high standard. Aged copper kickboards are then artificially aged using our specialised processes until they are the exact tone you’re looking for.

They are then protected with a tough transparent lacquer to both stop ageing and protect the finish.

Natural copper kickboards can be left bare metal or protected by the lacquer depending on your requirements.

All copper kitchen kickboards are created by hand, so every piece will be unique. They can also be created to your exact specifications, offering unrivalled freedom to design something that’s perfect for your needs!

Caring for your copper kitchen kickboards

The beauty of copper is not only in how it looks but also in how easy it is to care for.

Once installed in your kitchen, simply wash regularly with a soft cloth and warm soapy water. That’s all they need to remain looking as good as new throughout their lifetime!

Full care instructions are included with every order.
