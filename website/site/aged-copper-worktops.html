HAND-MADE AGED COPPER WORKTOPS

Copper is an amazing metal. It has been worked since time immemorial and features within interior designs for hundreds of years. Yet it still manages to appear modern and up to date.

You’ll often see aged copper worktops in kitchens, restaurants, bars, bistros, barber shops, salons and anywhere that wants to combine a statement piece with a practical surface.

All aged copper worktops begin life as sheets of copper . We laser cut the sheet to size and will either artificially age it or prepare the sheet for finishing.

We artificially age the copper using a specialist technique and halt the process once we reach the patina you ordered.

Once aged, we protect the copper worktop with tough transparent lacquer to help preserve that lovely finish.

Natural copper worktops can be left as bare metal or protected by the same lacquer.

As everything is made by hand and no two pieces will be the same. Many manufacturers offer uniqueness as a selling point, but Halman Thompson guarantees it!

CARING FOR YOUR AGED COPPER WORKTOP

Another benefit of copper is that it is so easy to look after.

Simply wipe clean with warm soapy water and a soft cloth and your worktop should look as good as new.

No need for specialist cleaning products or expensive solutions here!

All aged copper worktops come with full care instructions.
