About this piece

Our Blackened Steel represents the perfect harmony of raw industrial character and refined modernity. Crafted through a meticulous oxidation and sealing process, each panel reveals a distinctive patina – deep charcoal tones and smokey greys.

Lead times for our samples are 2-3 days from order.

Every piece is sealed with a protective lacquer or crystalline wax to keep the finish as it is. If that layer is worn away by harsh cleaning or heat, the metal will carry on ageing naturally.

  • Clean gently with warm, soapy water
  • Wipe up spills straight away — acidic liquids like vinegar or fruit juice will mark the finish
  • Use heat mats and avoid scratching
  • Re-lacquer if needed
