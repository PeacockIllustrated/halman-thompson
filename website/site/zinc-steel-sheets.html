Zinc and Steel Sheets

Zinc or steel is a great choice for those seeking a material that combines durability with a sleek, modern aesthetic. All our sheets come lacquered as standard to protect the metal from further ageing and maintain a consistent appearance over time. For those who prefer the natural patina process, we can also provide the material without lacquer that allows the zinc or steel to develop more character as it ages. Zinc or steel can easily complement a variety of styles, from industrial chic to minimalist elegance. With stunning finishes to suit your style, including lightly aged zinc , antique zinc , corten steel – weathered and blackened steel , these metals are the perfect choice for both domestic and commercial applications.

Why Use Zinc or Steel Sheets?

Zinc or steel sheet metal offers numerous advantages that make it an appealing option for many different projects. Its natural strength and resistance to corrosion ensure long-lasting performance, whether used indoors or outdoors. They are also known for its low environmental impact, making it a more sustainable and eco-friendly material choice. Beyond its durability, they are lightweight, cost-effective, and requires little maintenance, which adds to its appeal. Thanks to its versatility, zinc or stell sheets can be applied across domestic and commercial settings alike – from crafting and decorative projects like zinc tiles, custom lettering, tabletops, and decorative wall panels, to more industrial applications such as building facades, roofing, and cladding.

At Halman Thompson, we take care of all the necessary preparation and finishing to ensure your sheet is custom-made to meet your exact requirements. This approach guarantees the highest quality and finish, ensuring the material is perfect for your project and preserves the appearance we’re proud to deliver.
