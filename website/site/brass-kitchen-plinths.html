HAND CRAFTED BRASS KITCHEN PLINTHS

All brass kitchen plinths are created by hand by expert artisans. We have years of experience delivering masterful metalwork so you’re in good hands!

All brass kitchen plinths begin as sheets of brass . We then precision cut to the exact shape and size you specify using a laser.

Next, we prepare the metal by artificially ageing it or polishing if you’re going for natural brass. Once ready, the plinth can be protected by a tough transparent lacquer to preserve the look for years to come.

Every brass kitchen plinth will be unique but our team will ensure they are all uniform and complement each other while also showing their own character.

Ordering is simple. Complete the order form or contact one of our team . Tell us what you want, the size, finish, and number of plinths and we’ll do the rest!

CARING FOR YOUR BRASS KITCHEN PLINTHS

Not only do our brass kitchen plinths look amazing, they are very easy to maintain too!

Simply wipe down with a soft cloth and warm soapy water as part of your normal cleaning routine. No need for special processes or chemicals as the lacquer protects the metal.

Full care instructions are included with every order.
