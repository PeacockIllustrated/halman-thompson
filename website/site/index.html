How it works

From a 150mm square to a finished wall

  1. 1 · Sample

    See it in your own light

    Every sample is a 150 × 150mm piece of 0.9mm metal, finished by hand.

    £26.34 · 2–3 working days

  2. 2 · Measure

    Send us your sizes

    We cut to the millimetre, with cut-outs for sockets and pipes on request.

    0.9mm or 1.5mm sheet

  3. 3 · Make

    Aged by hand to order

    Each piece is finished in our workshop and sealed in gloss or matte lacquer.

    5–8 weeks · delivery included

  4. 4 · Fit

    Ready to go up

    Pieces arrive ready to fit to most flat surfaces with epoxy resin or liquid nail.

    UK mainland and international
