Verdigris Copper Kitchen Backsplash

The project

This verdigris copper kitchen splashback project is an exercise in how to use aged copper in the home. It’s a modest piece but punches way above its weight in terms of design and aesthetics!

The customer wanted large swathes of verdigris within the copper, which our team was able to deliver.

As each piece we create is made by hand, we can deliver almost any finish in any shape or size. This copper kitchen splashback was laser cut to size, artificially aged and then finished to deliver what we’re sure you’ll agree is an awesome statement piece!
