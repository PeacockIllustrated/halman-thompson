Hand crafted metal worktops

All metal worktops ordered through Halman Thompson are manufactured by hand to your specific requirements.

All metal worktops will be laser cut to your exact measurements to ensure a seamless fit.

Aged copper and aged brass use a chemical process to create the look. We do this by hand so we can fully control the end result. Once done, we stop the ageing process and seal the finish in a tough, transparent lacquer to protect it.

That same lacquer can be applied to natural copper and natural brass worktops but that is purely optional.

Caring for your metal worktop

Caring for your metal worktop is simple. Keep it clean with warm soapy water and a soft cloth. That’s all there is to it.

Full care instructions are provided with every metal worktop.
