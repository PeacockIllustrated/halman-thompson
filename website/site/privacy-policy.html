Privacy Policy

Last updated: 5 November 2026

Who we are

This website, https://halmanthompson.com , is run by Halman Thompson Ltd, Newburn Industrial Estate, 26-29 Shelley Road, Newcastle upon Tyne NE15 9RT (“we”, “us”). We are the data controller for the personal data described in this policy. If you have any questions about it, email sales@halmanthompson.com or call 0191 250 9853.

We process personal data in line with the UK General Data Protection Regulation (UK GDPR), the Data Protection Act 2018 and the Privacy and Electronic Communications Regulations (PECR).

What personal data we collect and why

Enquiry and quote forms

When you use our contact, bespoke quote, signage or other enquiry forms we collect your name, email address, phone number, the product type and finish you are interested in, your message and any files you choose to upload (such as drawings or photos). We use this to reply to your enquiry, prepare a quote and, if you go ahead, carry out the work. Our lawful basis is taking steps at your request before entering into a contract, and our legitimate interest in responding to enquiries.

Form submissions are stored in our website database and sent to our sales team by email. Please do not include sensitive information (for example health details) in the message or uploaded files.

Orders and accounts

When you buy from our online shop we collect your name, billing and delivery address, email address, phone number, order details and any notes you add. If you create an account we also store your login details and order history. We use this to process, deliver and support your order, to send order and delivery updates and to meet our tax and accounting obligations. Our lawful basis is performance of a contract and compliance with legal obligations.

Payments (Stripe)

Card and wallet payments (including Apple Pay and Google Pay where offered) are processed by Stripe Payments Europe Ltd. Your card details are entered into fields hosted by Stripe and go directly to Stripe; we never see or store your full card number. Stripe receives your payment details, name, email, billing address, IP address and device information, and uses them to process the payment and to prevent fraud. Stripe acts as an independent controller for its fraud-prevention activities. See Stripe’s privacy policy .

Reviews

After an order is completed we may share your name, email address and order reference with Trustpilot so it can invite you to review your purchase. Leaving a review is optional. Our lawful basis is our legitimate interest in collecting genuine customer feedback. See Trustpilot’s privacy terms .

Live chat

If you use the live chat on our site, the chat provider (LiveChat) processes the messages you send, any name or email you give, and technical information such as your IP address and browser, so that our team can reply. Chat transcripts are kept so we can follow up on your enquiry.

Embedded content

Some pages include content from other websites, such as our Instagram feed, Google reviews and Trustpilot widgets. Embedded content behaves as if you had visited the other website, and those websites may collect data about you, use cookies and track your interaction with the content, particularly if you are logged in to them.

Cookies

We use cookies and similar technologies. Strictly necessary cookies keep the site working, for example remembering your basket, keeping you logged in, protecting the checkout and remembering your cookie choices. These are set without consent because the site cannot work without them.

Analytics and advertising cookies are only set if you accept them in our cookie banner. You can change or withdraw your choice at any time using the cookie settings link on the site, or by clearing cookies in your browser. Our Cookie Policy lists the cookies we use and how long they last.

Analytics and advertising

With your consent we use the following tools, loaded through Google Tag Manager, to understand how the site is used and to measure our advertising:

  • Google Analytics (Google Ireland Ltd) records pages viewed, products viewed and added to the basket, checkout steps and completed purchases (order reference, value, tax, shipping and products), along with device, browser and approximate location. We do not send your name, email address or address to Google Analytics.
  • Matomo analytics records similar visit and shop activity so we can see how the site and shop are performing.
  • Meta Pixel (Meta Platforms Ireland Ltd) records visits and conversions, such as enquiries and purchases, so we can measure and improve our adverts on Facebook and Instagram and show relevant adverts to people who have visited our site.
  • Google Ads may record conversions from our adverts.

Our lawful basis for analytics and advertising is your consent. We work with a marketing agency that manages our advertising campaigns and has access to these reports. You can opt out at any time through the cookie settings, and you can manage ad preferences in your Meta and Google accounts.

Who we share your data with

We do not sell your personal data. We share it only where needed with:

  • our website hosting, email delivery and security providers;
  • Stripe, for payments;
  • delivery and courier companies, for orders being delivered;
  • Trustpilot, LiveChat, Google, Meta and Matomo as described above;
  • our accountants, professional advisers and HMRC where required by law.

Some of these providers are based outside the UK, including in the EU and the USA. Where data is transferred outside the UK we rely on UK adequacy regulations, the UK Extension to the EU-US Data Privacy Framework or the UK International Data Transfer Agreement / Addendum to protect it.

How long we keep your data

  • Enquiries and quotes that do not lead to an order: up to 2 years from our last contact.
  • Orders, invoices and related correspondence: 6 years after the end of the financial year, as required for tax and accounting.
  • Customer accounts: until you ask us to close the account, or after 3 years of inactivity.
  • Analytics data: up to 14 months.

Your rights

You have the right to ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, and to ask us to correct, delete or restrict it, to object to our processing it, or to have it transferred to another organisation. Where we rely on consent you can withdraw it at any time. Some data we must keep for legal reasons. To use any of these rights, email sales@halmanthompson.com . We will reply within one month.

If you are unhappy with how we have handled your data you can complain to the Information Commissioner’s Office at ico.org.uk or on 0303 123 1113, though we would appreciate the chance to put things right first.

How we protect your data

The site is served over an encrypted (HTTPS) connection, payments are handled by Stripe, and access to our website and email systems is restricted to staff who need it. We keep our software up to date and use security monitoring to protect against attacks.

Data breaches

If a personal data breach occurs that is likely to put your rights at risk, we will report it to the ICO within 72 hours and let you know without undue delay where required.

Automated decision making

We do not make decisions about you based solely on automated processing.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.
