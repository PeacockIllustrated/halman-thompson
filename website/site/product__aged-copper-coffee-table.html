Description

Elevate your living space with our exquisite aged copper coffee table – a unique fusion of functionality and style that showcases the wonderful rosy glow of copper and the expert craftsmanship behind it.

A natural copper coffee table isn’t just a piece of furniture; it’s an opportunity to make a lasting impression in your home.

Our handmade aged copper coffee tables serve as not just practical pieces but also as conversation starters, designed to enhance the aesthetic appeal and practicality of your living space. Each table is handmade in our studio from natural copper, hand-aged to your taste and fitted on MDF with powder-coated steel legs in black.

When you choose one of our aged copper coffee tables, you’re not just selecting a piece of furniture; you’re commissioning a personalised work of art. Handcrafted to order, each table undergoes a meticulous ageing process that we tailor to your vision by hand, ensuring that the final result exceeds your expectations.

Available in three sizes – Small (1220 x 610 x 405mm), Medium (1325 x 710 x 450mm), and Large (1375 x 760 x 500mm) – these tables cater to various spatial requirements. Select from our range of copper styles and finishes to make the piece your own – there’s something to suit every décor.

As with all our aged metal creations, we will share our progress with you. Once you’re happy with the level of ageing achieved, we will seal your bespoke aged copper coffee table using your choice of gloss or matte lacquer to add the perfect touch of refinement. This not only protects the table but also enhances its aesthetic appeal, ensuring it remains a stunning focal point for years to come.

The versatile style of this handcrafted coffee table also makes it an excellent addition to commercial spaces such as cafes, offices, or waiting rooms.

Whether it’s a centrepiece in your living room, a chic bedroom accent, or a stylish addition to a commercial space, a customised aged copper coffee table will complement your interior design and add a touch of sophistication wherever it’s placed.

Please allow 8-10 weeks for our artisans to make your table.
