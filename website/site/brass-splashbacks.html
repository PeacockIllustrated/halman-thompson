Brass Splashbacks

Splashbacks are a modest part of a kitchen but can be incredibly influential when included within an interior design. One way to make them really stand out is with a brass kitchen splashback.

You will often see brass splashbacks in interior design schemes, in magazines, commercial kitchen projects and anywhere someone wants to make an impact with a statement feature.

All brass splashbacks from Halman Thompson are bespoke and made to order. You can request a mix of natural or aged brass finish and have it cut to any size or shape.

Please note: our pieces are suitable for use behind non-steam cookers only. It is important that the material is not exposed to direct contact with steam or excessive heat, as this will damage the surface finish.

BESPOKE BRASS SPLASHBACKS

Aged brass splashbacks use an artificial ageing process to deliver their signature look. Our experts will age your splashback to the desired level, stop the ageing and seal it in lacquer.

Natural brass finishes can be left as is or also protected by lacquer.

All brass splashbacks can be laser cut to any size or shape. For larger areas, multiple sheets could be used next to each other for maximum effect. We would age all sheets together to get the patina as close as possible too.

Standard squares and rectangles can be ordered directly. Other shapes and custom sizes can be provided via quotation.

MOUNTING BRASS SPLASHBACKS

Fitting your brass splashback is easy. The sheets are light enough to be able to be fixed into place using epoxy or adhesive.

Prepare the wall to make sure it’s sealed and flat, apply the adhesive to the back of the splashback, offer it up to the wall and adjust until it’s perfectly aligned. Then leave until the adhesive dries.

That’s it!

CARING FOR YOUR BRASS SPLASHBACK

Looking after your brass splashback is simple, clean using warm soapy water and a soft sponge or cloth. Avoid using abrasive cleaners or scourers to not accelerate lacquer wear.

Full cleaning and care instructions will be provided with every splashback.
