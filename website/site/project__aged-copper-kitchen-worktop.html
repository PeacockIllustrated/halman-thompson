Aged Copper Kitchen Worktop

Metal
Copper
Type
Worktops
Made
Newcastle upon Tyne

Using a 1.5mm copper sheet, we precision cut the sheet into shape with a laser, use our specialist ageing processes to deliver a rich verdigris patina in Northumberland finish and shaped it around a custom-made wooden counter.

The project

The effect is stunning. What is usually a forgettable piece of countertop is now the star of the show. It contrasts the area around it while offering a practical, yet attractive surface for the sink.

We, and the client, couldn’t be happier with the result! Find out more on our copper worktops page.
