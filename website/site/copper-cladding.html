Copper Cladding

Hand crafted copper cladding sheets

Copper cladding is an incredibly flexible material ideal for building and decorating. It can be cut to any shape or size and be prepared to your exact specifications.

Every copper cladding sheet is made by hand, by experienced artisans so you can choose the finish you want.

Copper cladding finishes include:

  • Cheshire – The Cheshire aged copper finish is dark, rich and decadent, ideal for making a statement.
  • Northumberland – The Northumberland aged copper finish has swirls of verdigris throughout.
  • Hertfordshire – The Hertfordshire finish has more angular verdigris elements that catch the eye.
  • Ayrshire – The Ayrshire aged copper finish has a lovely, speckled effect that’s constantly surprising.
  • Hampshire – The Hampshire aged copper finish has a rust-like finish that expresses itself well.
  • Corten – The Corten steel finish is incredibly popular in gardens and architecture and looks amazing. It has a rich copper colour but is in fact made of steel.
  • Natural copper – The natural lustre of copper laid bare for everyone to appreciate both indoors and out.

Unique copper cladding

Every order is created by hand, using tried and tested techniques.

Your copper sheet is first cut to size and prepared for use. Depending on the finish you choose, we will then artificially age the copper until it meets your requirements.

We then stop the ageing process and seal the sheet in a tough, transparent lacquer. This prevents further ageing and protects the sheet from the elements and daily use.

Natural copper can also be lacquered or left depending on the use. For indoor use, lacquer is purely optional. For outdoor use, copper cladding can be left unlacquered, this allows the copper to change patina from shinny copper to a range of greens.

Caring for your copper cladding

Another reason copper cladding is so popular is ease of use. It can be installed using adhesive or fixings and requires minimal maintenance once in place.

The lacquer protects the copper and requires a simple clean with warm soapy water to keep it looking amazing.

Full care instructions are included with every order so you’ll know exactly what to do.
